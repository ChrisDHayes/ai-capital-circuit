THE RETURN OF DEMAND

Demand is the economic test.

How does capital investment translate into usage, recognized revenue and cash returns?

A contract is a promise to transact over time; revenue is recognized under accounting rules; physical capacity must be constructed and used. The gap between them is central to the AI financing debate.

Read the deeper research ↗Read the article ↗
WHAT THE EVIDENCE SHOWS

Company disclosures document large backlogs and growing realized revenue alongside infrastructure expansion.

WHAT WE CANNOT ASSUME

Backlog, annual revenue, GPU capacity and installed megawatts cannot be added or compared as equivalent dollar flows.

INSPECT
CoreWeave filings
05 / Promises versus realization

Four numbers. Four different economic clocks.

CoreWeave's 2026 filings illustrate why contracted backlog, realized sales and physical capacity cannot be collapsed into a single financial-flow measure.

Don't add incomparable amounts

Revenue is recognized over a reporting period; backlog is future expected revenue; active power and contracted power measure physical capacity. These figures can coexist without implying that the same cash was transferred twice.

Evidence constraints that remain

Operating waterfall ≠ legal recovery

Ordinary permitted payments, post-enforcement application and final outcomes in insolvency are distinct analytical states. Other priority rules, netting, claims validity and collateral realizations can affect legal outcomes.

Instrument size ≠ cash available

A delayed-draw commitment is not a funded balance. Debt coupons depend on principal outstanding, dates, floating-rate resets and hedges. Missing schedules remain unknown rather than smoothed over time.

Every consequential number needs a receipt.

Source links are part of the analysis, not decoration. The record preserves its financial meaning, reporting date and caveat, allowing viewers to challenge any headline claim.

How is this prototype sourced and constrained?

The transaction records and network are drawn from Stage 5's deal lifecycle, graph edges and source registry; the bubble panel is taken from Stage 4's company financial reconstruction. The interface runs offline and does not fetch live prices or update reports automatically.

Where cash settlement, contract utilization, financing maturity or customer-level cash flows are undisclosed, the prototype does not infer them. Primary-document URLs open in the browser only when the device is connected.

Revenue run rates, valuation, company-wide capital expenditure, contractual commitments and debt balances belong to different accounting categories. Fiscal-year series cannot be treated as AI-only or strict calendar-year figures.
